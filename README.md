# 💫 About Me:

Hi 👋, I'm Sayee

My work centers on translating technical findings, control gaps, regulatory requirements, and operational risks into clear business impact, measurable risk, and practical remediation priorities.

🔭 I’m currently building hands-on projects around Enterprise Risk Management, cybersecurity GRC, AI governance, third-party risk, and public-sector technology risk.
