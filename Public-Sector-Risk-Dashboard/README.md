# Public Sector Strategic Risk & Resilience Dashboard

An interactive enterprise risk management dashboard built with Python and Streamlit to demonstrate how cybersecurity, technology, operational, and governance risks can be translated into measurable risk exposure and executive decision support.

---

## Project Objective

The goal of this project is to show how organizations can move beyond simply identifying cybersecurity findings and instead connect them to:

- Business impact
- Inherent risk
- Control effectiveness
- Residual risk
- Risk appetite
- Key Risk Indicators
- Maturity gaps
- Risk treatment decisions
- Remediation priorities
- Executive reporting

The dashboard is designed around public-sector technology and cybersecurity scenarios, but the underlying risk-management approach can be applied across regulated environments.

---

## Key Capabilities

The dashboard includes:

- Strategic Risk Register
- Inherent Risk Scoring
- Residual Risk Calculation
- Risk Appetite Monitoring
- Key Risk Indicator Tracking
- Maturity Gap Analysis
- Risk Treatment Recommendations
- Remediation Prioritization
- POA&M-Style Tracking
- Executive Risk Metrics
- Interactive Filtering
- Detailed Risk Narratives
